WiPaws

Elite Pet Valet

WiPaws treats your dog with the same care you would. Our pet valets arrive for a brisk walk in the neighborhood or adjacent trails, using secure harnesses and GPS-tracked routes. After the walk, paws are wiped with natural-fiber wipes to protect your floors, the water bowl is refreshed, and a high-quality photo is sent to your phone. Proof of life, every time.

What's Included

  • Brisk neighborhood walk
  • Paw wiping & hygiene
  • Hydration refill
  • Photo proof of life
  • GPS-tracked routes
  • Secure harness (no collar)

Why Choose WiPaws

  • 20-30 minute walks in upscale neighborhoods
  • Natural-fiber paw wipes — safe for pets and floors
  • Automated SMS update after every walk
  • Always high-quality harness — never just a collar
