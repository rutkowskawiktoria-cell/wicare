WiGarden

Architectural Botanical Care

WiGarden keeps your architectural plants thriving. Luxury homes feature expensive, large-scale greenery — fiddle leaf figs, monsteras, orchids — that standard cleaners avoid and specialist plant services charge a premium for. WiGarden bridges that gap, providing expert care: gentle leaf dusting, moisture meter checks to prevent root rot, precision watering, dead leaf removal with sterilized shears, and 90-degree pot rotation for even growth.

What's Included

  • Leaf dusting & cleaning
  • Moisture meter check
  • Precision watering
  • Aesthetic pruning
  • Pot rotation
  • Plant health assessment

Why Choose WiGarden

  • Professional moisture meter — never guess
  • Sterilized pruning shears for every cut
  • 90-degree rotation for even sunlight
  • Monthly plant health report
